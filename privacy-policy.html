Privacy Policy

Monster Rush Arena · Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Monster Rush Arena (the “Game”), published by the developer operating the anishacharya95 GitHub profile (“we”, “us” or “our”), handles information when you play the Game.

1. Information the Game stores on your device

The Game saves gameplay progress and preferences in local browser storage on your device. This may include your coins, unlocked and selected heroes, unlocked and selected arenas, achievements, defeated monsters and bosses, gameplay statistics, daily-challenge progress, audio levels, graphics quality and vibration preferences.

The Game does not require you to create an account. The reviewed game code does not send this local save data to our own game servers and does not provide cloud saves or cross-device synchronisation. Local save data remains on your device unless you reset it in Settings, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Device backup behaviour may depend on Android and your device settings.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Android release integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and can request app-open and interstitial advertisements. When ads are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information from the app or device, such as the advertising ID or other device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, ad views and interactions, and fraud-prevention, diagnostics or performance information. The precise data and purposes can vary based on your device, region, consent choices, ad settings and Google's services.

Google processes advertising information under its own terms and privacy practices. We do not control Google's advertising systems or independently determine all information collected by the AdMob SDK. Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about how AdMob uses data for details.

You can review available advertising and privacy controls in your Android device settings and Google's privacy settings. Resetting or limiting an advertising identifier may affect ad personalisation, but does not necessarily stop all advertising or all data processing by the ad provider.

3. Information we do not ask you to provide

The Game does not include player registration, a player profile, or a game account in the reviewed release. It does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings or payment-card details to play. The Android manifest reviewed for this policy requests internet access for the Game's online advertising service; it does not declare location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone permissions.

The project contains a dependency that can support Google Gemini, but no active Gemini prompt-submission feature or Gemini request flow was identified in the reviewed game code. This policy therefore does not describe Gemini as an active Game feature. If that changes in a future release, this policy will be updated before the feature is introduced.

4. How information is used

Local gameplay information is used to save your progress and settings. Information handled by Google through AdMob may be used by Google and its partners to deliver, measure, protect against invalid activity, and improve advertising services, subject to Google's policies and your available privacy choices.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your locally stored gameplay save data. We do not operate a player-account database or a cloud-save service in the reviewed release. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners through AdMob as described in Google's policies. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights or safety of users or the Game.

6. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data stays in the app's local storage until you use the in-game Reset Save Data option, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Resetting or uninstalling the Game does not delete information that Google may already have processed through its advertising services. For requests about advertising information processed by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact Google.

Because the reviewed Game does not create player accounts or store game saves on our servers, there is no Game account or server-side save record for us to delete.

7. Children's privacy

The Game does not ask players to submit personal information or create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the Game's own gameplay features. The advertising service may process device or advertising information and applies its own age, consent and regional requirements. The app's audience and advertising settings must be configured accurately in Google Play Console and AdMob for the intended age groups and locations.

8. Security

Gameplay saves are stored in the app's local browser storage and are subject to the security of your device and Android environment. Network advertising requests are handled by Google Mobile Ads. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

  • Reset gameplay progress using Settings → Reset Save Data.
  • Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app using Android settings.
  • Review available advertising ID, ad personalisation and privacy controls in Android and Google settings.
  • Contact Google about information processed by Google's advertising services.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game's features, third-party services or applicable requirements change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about Monster Rush Arena, contact the developer through the anishacharya95 GitHub profile.
